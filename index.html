Who I Am

Industrial Designer & Educator

I completed my Industrial Design Honours degree at Western Sydney University and have spent 5 years working in the design industry. This means I don't just teach the HSC syllabus, I bring real-world design experience into every session.

I studied Design and Technology at HSC myself, so I understand the syllabus inside and out. More importantly, I know how to help students think like designers: problem-solving, iteration, and understanding the "why" behind design decisions.

Whether you're struggling with design processes, technical drawings, case studies, or your major project, we'll work through it together with practical examples from real design work.
